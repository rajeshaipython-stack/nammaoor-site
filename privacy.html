Privacy Policy

NammaOor (நம்மூர்) · Last updated: 28 July 2026

NammaOor ("we", "our", "the app") is a local shops directory for Tamil Nadu that connects customers with nearby shops through QR codes, product listings, offers, reviews and a shop-billing tool. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it is protected. By using NammaOor you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Shop owner details — shop name, owner name, mobile number, WhatsApp number, address, category, UPI ID, bank details (only for payout), and business photos that owners choose to add.
  • Customer details — when a customer follows a shop, writes a review, or places a WhatsApp order, we store the review text, rating, and an anonymous device identifier. We do not require customers to create an account or share their phone number to browse or follow shops.
  • Location — with your permission, we use your device location only to show shops near you ("Near Me"). Location is used at the moment of search and is not tracked in the background.
  • Usage data — basic counts such as QR scans, calls, and page views, used to give shop owners simple analytics.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display shop pages, products, offers, and reviews.
  • To show nearby shops when you use Near Me.
  • To let customers contact shops via call, WhatsApp, or UPI payment.
  • To provide shop owners with basic analytics about their own shop.
  • To process the shop subscription (₹1 registration, ₹99/month) through our payment partner.

3. Payments

Shop subscription payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI PIN, or bank passwords. Payment information is handled directly by the payment provider under their own security standards.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only:

  • Publicly on a shop's own page — but only the details the shop owner chooses to publish (shop name, contact, products, offers).
  • With service providers who help us operate the app (hosting, database, payment processing) under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law.

5. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on secured cloud servers. Images and media are stored on encrypted cloud storage. Passwords are stored using one-way encryption (hashing) and are never visible to us. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access.

6. Your Choices

  • Shop owners can edit or remove their shop details, products, and photos from the owner dashboard at any time.
  • Customers can delete their own review at any time.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission from your device settings; only Near Me will stop working.

7. Children

NammaOor is intended for general audiences and business use. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page with an updated date.
